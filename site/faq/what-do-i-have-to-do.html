What I never need

  • Your passwords or logins. Not for the scan and not for filing.
  • A credit card for the scan or the first removal.
  • Anything from when you were under 18. Don't send those to anyone: see below.

Before you sign

A copyright notice has to name the rights holder or their agent, and some recipients forward notices to the uploader or publish a copy. So ask me before we start, and I'll walk you through what each one sees. It's the one decision worth a few minutes: read who sees your name first.

If a link is on a page you licensed or it's someone who looks like you, tell me. Your own profiles, pages you licensed and lookalikes are never filed against, and you know those better than I do.

If you'd rather do it all yourself

That's a fair choice. See when DIY is enough.

Under 18, or the images were taken when you were under 18? Don't send them to anyone, me included. Take It Down, run by NCMEC, works from your own device: your file stays there and only a digital fingerprint is shared with participating platforms.

Quick answers

Do I have to send you my photos or videos?

The scan needs only your handle. Before I file anything I need the signed one-page authorization, and now and then a link to your original post so I can show it's yours.

Can I cancel if I change my mind?

The monthly plan can be cancelled anytime. The one-time clean never auto-renews: after 40 days nothing happens unless you say so.
