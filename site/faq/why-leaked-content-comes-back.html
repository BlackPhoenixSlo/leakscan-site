Why does leaked content come back?

Because a takedown removes one copy at one address. The file still exists on the devices of whoever saved it, and nothing stops them uploading it again. So a comeback usually doesn't mean the first notice failed. It means there's another copy.

Which kind of comeback is it?

What you seeWhat it probably isWhat to do
The same address works againA counter-notice, or the site restored itAsk the provider which. A counter-notice has a legal answer; a quiet restore gets a fresh notice
Same site, new addressA re-uploadFile a new notice, and mention it's a repeat
A new site with the same photosAnother copy, or a scraperTreat it as a new target: site, host, registrar
The old address is dead, but it's still in searchA stale result, not a comebackOutdated content request

What reduces repeats

Nothing ends them, but some tools do more than one link at a time:

  • Repeat infringer policies. A US provider relying on the safe harbor needs a policy to terminate repeat infringers in appropriate circumstances. If one account keeps uploading, name it in your notices.
  • Copy prevention on the platform. YouTube's Studio form has an option to prevent future copies being uploaded.
  • Duplicate removal in search. For sexual imagery requests, Google says it tries to find and remove duplicates, unless you opt out.
  • Known copies under the TAKE IT DOWN Act. Platforms must make reasonable efforts to remove known identical copies after a valid request.
  • Fingerprints. StopNCII lets participating platforms look for matches, though only on those platforms.

Re-checking

If you're doing it yourself, re-check every few weeks and re-send for any new copies. Keep a list of what you filed, with dates, so a repeat is easy to prove. Searching the way this page describes takes a few minutes.

Quick answers

Does a re-upload mean my first takedown failed?

Usually not. A takedown works on one copy at one address. A re-upload is a new copy, and it needs its own notice.

Can content be removed permanently?

Nobody can honestly promise that. A takedown can't reach a file already saved to someone's device. What you can do is make re-uploads quick to catch and quick to file against.
