Why I Built This Site, and Why It Is the Only One I Control

Hi, it’s me. For a long time the only places you could find me were inside other people’s apps. My profiles, my photos, my messages: all of it lived on platforms that can change their rules overnight, hide my posts or lock my account without telling me why.

I didn’t plan to build a website. I planned to keep making content and answering messages, the way I always had. What changed my mind was a message from one of my subscribers. He had found what he thought was my new account. Same photos, same bio, one extra letter in the name. It had been running for weeks, and it was asking people for money.

That was the day I understood something simple: if I don’t have one place that is clearly mine, anyone can pretend to be me, and you have no easy way to check.

One address that is really mine

So this is it: the one address I own outright. Nobody can suspend it, nobody can bury it in a feed, and nobody else can post on it.

Every account I really use is listed on my official links page. If an account is not on that list, it is not me, however convincing it looks.

I also wanted somewhere calmer. Apps are built to keep you scrolling. This site is built to answer three questions quickly: who I am, where to find me, and how to tell the real me from a copy.

What you will find here

  • The official links: every account I actually run, kept up to date.
  • The verify page: how to check whether a profile, a message or a “new account” is really me.
  • This blog: the occasional post about my work and my week, written by me.

About leaks, briefly

Anyone who shares their work online learns this sooner or later: some of it ends up where it was never meant to be. Reposted, resold, cut up and re-uploaded under my name. I won’t pretend that doesn’t sting. But I would rather spend my energy on the people who support me properly than on the people who don’t.

So I have help with that part now. Copies get reported and taken down, fake profiles get flagged, and I get to go back to the part of this job I actually enjoy.

If an account is not on my official list, it is not me, however convincing it looks.

If you found me through a repost, a screenshot or a friend, welcome. Bookmark this page. If an account ever looks a little off, this is the place to check.

Thank you for being here.

Orla Brennick

Studio potter from Galway, Ireland. This is the only website she controls.
