⚠ I do not have a shop account

Since February 2026 I have found eleven accounts pretending to be me: seven on Instagram, four on TikTok. Most call themselves my shop, my studio or my "official" page. They post photos from my kiln-opening sales and ask for bank transfers in DMs. None of them are mine. I have no shop account, no backup and no second page, and I never take payment through messages.

⚠ "Free" glaze notebooks and giveaway prizes are scams

My firing logs and glaze notebook are only on Patreon. A site offering them as a "free download" is using my name as bait for card details or malware, and a message saying you won one of my pots and only need to pay shipping is not from me. Don't click, don't pay, and please report it.

That's the whole list. If it isn't here, it isn't me, no matter whose pots are on it.
