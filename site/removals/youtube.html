How do I remove leaked content from YouTube?

YouTube is unusual here: it has four separate routes, and the first one that often applies doesn't involve copyright at all. Its policy says explicit content meant to be sexually gratifying isn't allowed on YouTube, and it names sexualized material shared without consent, including nude photo leaks.

The copyright removal request

The copyright owner, or someone authorized such as an attorney, files it. For videos, YouTube offers a form in Studio, with email, fax and mail as alternatives. The request asks for your contact details, a description of the work, direct links, the good-faith and perjury statements, and your full legal name as a signature.

Three things worth knowing first:

  • What the uploader sees. Your name, email and description of the work may be shared with them. YouTube says your physical address and phone number stay confidential unless a lawsuit requires them.
  • Timing. You can schedule a request to take effect in 7 days, giving the uploader time to act. For a leak you'd normally leave that off.
  • Copies. The Studio form has an option to prevent future copies of the same content being uploaded.

If the uploader pushes back

They can send a counter notification. YouTube then gives the claimant 10 US business days to respond with evidence of legal action, and otherwise reinstates the video. Here's what that means for you.

Quick answers

Is a YouTube copyright request public?

Not in full, but YouTube says your name, email and description of the work may be shared with the uploader. Your physical address and phone number stay confidential unless a lawsuit requires them.

Can I report a whole channel?

Yes. YouTube's nudity and sexual content policy describes reporting a channel as well as a video. A channel set up to pose as you goes through the impersonation report form instead.
